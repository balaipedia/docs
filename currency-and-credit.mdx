## Currency

Every account has exactly one currency, `USD` or `IDR`. It is chosen when your account is created and cannot be changed afterwards. All monetary values (`balance`, `price_per_order`, `amount`, `required`) are in that currency, and every response that contains money also contains a `currency` field.

| Currency | Format | Example |
|----------|--------|---------|
| USD | 2 decimals | `0.15` = 15 cents |
| IDR | whole rupiah, no decimals | `2500` = Rp 2.500 |

Amounts are JSON numbers, not strings. Do not hard-code a currency symbol: read `currency` from the response.

<Note>
  Examples in this documentation use `USD`. This only affects the sample numbers: for an `IDR` account the same fields contain whole rupiah (see the table above). Your real currency is set on your account and is returned as `currency` in every response.
</Note>

## Credit and pricing

You top up a credit balance in your account currency. Each order costs your agreed price per order, deducted when the order is accepted.

- If an email address **fails the quick checks** (invalid format, domain typo, domain that cannot receive email, disposable, known-bad address) the request fails with `422`, no order is created and **no credit is charged**.
- If the deeper mailbox check fails right after the order was accepted (for example the mailbox does not exist or is full), the order ends as `error` within moments and the charge is **refunded automatically** (`refunded: true`). See [How an order is processed](/api-reference/create-order#how-an-order-is-processed).
- If we cannot process an order for an internal reason (`503`), no credit is charged either.
- If your balance is too low, the request fails with `402`.
