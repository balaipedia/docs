---
title: "Get balance"
description: "Your credit balance and price per order."
api: "GET https://orders.balaipedia.com/v1/balance"
---

Your current credit balance and your price per order, in your account currency. See [Currency and credit](/currency-and-credit).

<RequestExample>

```bash curl
curl https://orders.balaipedia.com/v1/balance \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY"
```

</RequestExample>

<ResponseExample>

```json 200 OK
{"object":"balance","balance":42.35,"currency":"USD","price_per_order":0.15}
```

</ResponseExample>
