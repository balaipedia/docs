---
title: "Top up with USDT"
description: "Start a USDT (BEP20) top-up. Returns the wallet address and exact amount to pay."
api: "POST https://orders.balaipedia.com/v1/topups"
---

Add credit automatically by paying USDT on the **BNB Smart Chain (BEP20)** network. No other network or coin is accepted. Credit is added by itself a few minutes after the payment is confirmed on chain.

## Body

<ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
  The credit you want to receive. For a `USD` account, `amount` is dollars with at most 2 decimals; for an `IDR` account it is whole rupiah.
</ParamField>

## Rules

- **Send exactly `pay.amount`, including the last 3 decimals.** The small extra digits identify your payment, and they are credited to you too (`credit_on_payment` already includes them). A different amount cannot be matched automatically.
- If your exchange or wallet takes a withdrawal fee, add it on top so that the full `pay.amount` arrives.
- **IDR accounts:** the credit is the USDT received multiplied by `exchange_rate.per_usdt`, which is fixed when the top-up is created.
- `status` is `pending`, `paid` or `expired`. A top-up is valid for **6 hours**; after that it is `expired` and no longer checked. A payment sent after it expired is not credited automatically (contact support with the transaction hash). A payment sent before it expired is still credited a few minutes after it is confirmed on chain.
- Creating a top-up with the same amount while one is still open returns the existing one with `200` instead of `201`. At most 3 top-ups can be open at once.
- Poll [`GET /topups/{id}`](/api-reference/get-topup) to see `paid`. The credit also appears in [transactions](/api-reference/list-transactions) with `topup_id`.

## Errors

| HTTP | `code` | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | `invalid_request` | `field: "amount"`, `reason`: `too_small` or `too_large`. |
| 429 | `too_many_pending_topups` | Too many open top-ups. |
| 503 | `service_unavailable` | Service unavailable. |
| 503 | `rate_unavailable` | IDR exchange rate temporarily unavailable, `Retry-After: 300`. |
| 503 | `temporarily_unavailable` | `Retry-After: 30`. |

```bash curl
curl -X POST https://orders.balaipedia.com/v1/topups \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount": 25}'
```

</RequestExample>
