---

There is no separate sandbox or test mode: every accepted `POST /orders` sends a real invitation and is charged at your normal price. Test in this order, from the cheapest step to the real one:

1. [`GET /ping`](/api-reference/ping): confirms you can reach the API (no key needed).
2. [`GET /balance`](/api-reference/get-balance): confirms your key works (`401` means it does not).
3. [`POST /webhook`](/api-reference/set-webhook) then [`POST /webhook/test`](/api-reference/test-webhook): confirms your endpoint is reachable and your signature check is correct, without creating an order. You should get `{"delivered":true}`.
4. One real order to an email address you control: confirms the full flow (`201 processing`, then `order.success` or a status you read with [`GET /orders/{id}`](/api-reference/get-order)). It costs one order.

Before going live, also try a request with a wrong key (`401`) and a repeated `Idempotency-Key` with the same email (the same order comes back and you are not charged twice).
